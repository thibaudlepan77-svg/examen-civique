French civic exam 2026, the practical steps

Registering for the French civic exam, price, centres and results

Where to register, what it costs, what to bring, when the result arrives and who is exempt. Each point links to the official page that says it, read on September 28, 2026.

Two approved bodies, and you register with them

The ministry website informs and helps you prepare, it registers nobody. You sign up with one of the two approved bodies, each with its own test centres and dates.

« Deux organismes ont été agréés par le ministère de l’intérieur pour la mise en œuvre de l’examen civique »

Two bodies have been approved by the Ministry of the Interior to run the civic exam.

Ministry of the Interior, read on September 28, 2026 see the page
  • CCI Paris Île-de-France (CCIP). Session search by city and date, for all three levels, multi-year residence permit (carte de séjour pluriannuelle), resident card (carte de résident) and naturalisation.
  • France Éducation International (FEI). Registration form, which asks for your département, your city and then the centre. Map of FEI centres.

Both sites are in French. On the CCIP search, the exam is listed as Examen civique mention Carte de séjour pluriannuelle, mention Carte de résident or mention Naturalisation.

How much the civic exam costs

There is no national price. Each centre sets its own.

« Les droits d’inscription sont fixés par le centre de passation. Les tarifs peuvent varier d’un centre à l’autre. »

Registration fees are set by the test centre. Prices may vary from one centre to another.

Service-public.fr, read on September 28, 2026 see the page

One centre that publishes its price, the Alliance française in Aix-Marseille, charges 80 euros.

« Le prix de l’inscription à l’examen civique s’élève à 80,00€ »

The registration fee for the civic exam is 80.00 euros.

Alliance française Aix-Marseille Provence, read on September 28, 2026 see the page

Elsewhere the amount only shows up during registration, so compare two or three nearby centres before paying. Sitting the exam again means registering, and paying, again. At CCIP centres a booking cannot be undone.

« Une inscription est ferme et définitive. Les droits d’inscription que vous avez versés restent acquis. »

A registration is firm and final. The fees you paid are not refunded.

CCI Paris Île-de-France, read on September 28, 2026 see the page

On the day

Arrive at the time on your notice (convocation). The invigilator checks your ID and the notice, asks you to confirm your name, date of birth and email, takes your photo and has you sign on a tablet. Check the spelling, it goes on your certificate.

« titre de séjour pour tout(e) candidat(e) ; passeport ou carte d’identité pour les ressortissants de l’Union européenne »

Residence permit for every candidate, passport or ID card for EU citizens.

CCI Paris Île-de-France, accepted ID, read on September 28, 2026 see the page

Phone off, no dictionary, no documents. Only your ID stays on the table.

The test itself is forty multiple choice questions in French, 28 on knowledge and 12 everyday scenarios, one right answer out of four, 45 minutes at most on a tablet or computer. You need 32 right. A wrong answer and a blank both score zero, so answer everything.

« bonne réponse = 1 point Pas de réponse ou mauvaise réponse = 0 point »

Right answer, 1 point. No answer or wrong answer, 0 points.

CCI Paris Île-de-France, results, read on September 28, 2026 see the page

Results and certificate

At CCIP centres the result comes by email within 24 to 48 working hours, with a link to an online vault where you download the certificate. There is no paper version.

« Vos résultats vous sont transmis dans un délai de 24h à 48h ouvrées, à partir du jour de passation de votre test. »

Your results are sent within 24 to 48 working hours from the day of your test.

CCI Paris Île-de-France, read on September 28, 2026 see the page

The certificate never expires. A pass at resident card level also counts for the multi-year residence permit.

« L’attestation de réussite à l’examen civique mention CR vaut attestation de réussite mention CSP, lors du dépôt de la demande de titre dans l’ANEF. »

A pass certificate at resident card level is valid as a pass at multi-year permit level when you apply on ANEF.

Ministry of the Interior, read on September 28, 2026 see the page

A serious incident during the test can be appealed within 30 days of the certificate being issued (CCIP rule). A fail is fixed by sitting it again, whenever you like and as often as you need.

Who must take it, who is exempt

Every adult foreign national who signed the republican integration contract (CIR) and applies for a first multi-year residence permit or a first resident card. For naturalisation by decree, you pass the exam before you apply, not after.

« Vous devez réussir l’examen civique avant de faire une demande de naturalisation française ou de réintégration dans la nationalité française par décret »

You must pass the civic exam before applying for French naturalisation or reinstatement in French nationality by decree.

Service-public.fr, read on September 28, 2026 see the page
  • Renewing a card you already hold does not require the certificate.
  • Beneficiaries of international protection and nationals covered by some bilateral agreements are not concerned, according to the ministry.
  • Foreign nationals over 65 do not have to pass it, according to CCIP, which gives this rule for residence permits.
  • People with a disability or a chronic illness can get adjustments, with a medical certificate.

Not sure your case fits? The préfecture and the centre you register with decide, not a preparation site, this one included.
