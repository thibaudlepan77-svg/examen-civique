In public schools, who is allowed to wear highly visible religious symbols?

An official question of the French civic exam, with its answer and the official text that proves it, in French and English.

Resident card no. 31

À l'école publique, qui peut porter des signes religieux très visibles ?

In public schools, who is allowed to wear highly visible religious symbols?

Answer. No one. Pupils are not allowed to wear conspicuous religious symbols, and teachers must remain neutral.

Personne. Les élèves n'ont pas le droit de porter de signes religieux ostensibles, et les enseignants doivent rester neutres.

Since the 2004 law, pupils in public primary schools, middle schools and high schools cannot wear a conspicuous religious symbol, meaning a symbol that clearly shows their religion. Teachers and staff must remain neutral and must not show their religion. Choose the option that says "no one", or the one saying that neither pupils nor teachers may do so.

The official text that proves it

« Dans les écoles, les collèges et les lycées publics, le port de signes ou tenues par lesquels les élèves manifestent ostensiblement une appartenance religieuse est interdit. »

In public primary schools, middle schools and high schools, the wearing of symbols or clothing by which pupils conspicuously display a religious affiliation is forbidden.
