According to Aboriginal legend, what created the colours of the opal?

  1. The light of the Southern Cross
  2. Gold found in the rivers
  3. Fire from a smoking ceremony
  4. A rainbow that touched the earth
Show the answer
D. A rainbow that touched the earth
An Aboriginal legend tells that a rainbow touched the earth and created the colours of the opal.
Source, Our Common Bond, Part 1, Australia and its people

In the first half of the 20th century, which migrants did programs actively encourage to settle in Australia?

  1. American migrants
  2. Indian migrants
  3. British migrants
  4. Chinese migrants
Show the answer
C. British migrants
In the first half of the 20th century, programs actively encouraged people from Britain to settle here, and many of them did.
